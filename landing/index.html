FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes — 100% offline after the initial one-time model download (~1 GB). AI models run locally on your CPU or GPU. No data leaves your machine.

What camera RAW formats are supported?

CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, ORF, RW2, RAF and more via the rawpy library (based on LibRaw). JPEG, PNG, and TIFF are also supported.

How fast is it?

On a modern 8-core CPU: ~200 photos/minute for culling, ~20 photos/minute for full pipeline. GPU acceleration (NVIDIA CUDA) speeds editing and super-resolution up to 5×.

Can I use the output in Lightroom or Capture One?

Yes. PhotoStudioHub writes Lightroom-compatible XMP sidecar files with star ratings (1–5) and color labels. Your selects open with the correct ratings already applied.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

The app stays installed and you keep your settings. You'll be prompted to enter a license key. No automatic charges — you choose when and what to buy.

Is the license per machine or per user?

Per machine (hardware-fingerprinted). One license = one computer. If you need multiple seats, contact us for a studio bundle.

What languages is the interface available in?

English, Deutsch, Hrvatski, Bosanski, Srpski — with more languages coming soon. The language system is open-architecture; any language can be added.
